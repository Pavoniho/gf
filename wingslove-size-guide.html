How our cups work

Your cup is the difference between your bust and your under bust — not your bust alone.

Between two sizes? Go up — our cups sit close together, so the larger one is almost always the better fit.

Wrong size? No problem! We offer free and hassle-free exchanges for a larger or smaller size.
